## <img src=".github/readme/reserve.svg" alt="" width="24" height="24" /> Pick your name

Visit [has-a.computer](https://has-a.computer) to request `yourname.has-a.computer`. Tell us what you're building and link your current site. We'll review your request and reply by email.

Please read the [terms](https://has-a.computer/terms) before sending your request.

## <img src=".github/readme/support.svg" alt="" width="24" height="24" /> Say hello

[Join us on Discord](https://discord.gg/mKQqsJ6UtK) for updates or a chat. Bug reports and ideas go in [issues](https://github.com/aspizu/has-a.computer/issues/new).

To report abuse, [email us](mailto:admin@has-a.computer) with the address and what's happening.
