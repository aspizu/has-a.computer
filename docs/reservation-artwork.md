## Final prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: a single isolated 3D illustration for the request-sent confirmation screen of a sky-blue website. Create a small cream-colored rounded mailbox with its sky-blue flag raised and one ivory envelope peeking out of its slightly open front door. The mailbox floats weightlessly, without a post or stand. Three-quarter view from slightly above, showing the open front and one side, with the raised flag clearly visible. Match the website's existing retro-computer illustration: warm ivory satin surfaces, softly rounded molded edges, subtle material texture, pale blue reflected light, bright soft studio lighting, gentle self-shadow, and a tangible polished 3D product-render style. Keep the form simple and readable when displayed at 176 pixels. The blue is close to #008aca; cream and ivory are the other main colors. Center the complete mailbox and its envelope in a square composition, filling about 80 percent of the image with comfortable margins. The background must be genuinely transparent with an alpha channel, including around the flag and door, so it can float over an existing sky image. No sky, clouds, ground, floor shadow, supporting pole, people, text, logo, checkmark, confetti, border, opaque backdrop, or baked checkerboard. Square PNG, approximately 1024 by 1024.
